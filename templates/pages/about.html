Why we built it

Students lose weeks practising the wrong things.

Most preparation apps are a long list of questions. Everyone gets the same ones in the same order, and it's up to the student to work out what's actually holding their score back.

We start from the other end: score every attempt, find the skill and question type that's costing you marks, and send you there next, at a level that stretches you without overwhelming you.
